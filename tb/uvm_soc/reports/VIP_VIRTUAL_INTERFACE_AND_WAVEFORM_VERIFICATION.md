## Executive Summary

This report establishes the verified configuration, retrieval, connectivity, and waveform observability of the three target Verification Intellectual Property (VIP) components:
1. **GPIO VIP** (32-bit bidirectional I/O with pin-level interrupts)
2. **UART VIP** (16550-compatible serial transceiver, 115200 Baud @ 40 MHz clock)
3. **PWM VIP** (Passive monitor-only analyzer for Real-Time Timer PWM waveform)

In strict compliance with architectural constraints:
- **No production RTL was modified** (`main_top.v` and peripheral RTL remain 100% read-only).
- **No non-project VIPs were introduced** (ADC, SPI, Watchdog, I2C are excluded).
- **PWM VIP architecture remains passive** (PWM is generated by the SoC's Real-Time Timer, not driven externally).
- **Observability signals are exposed in interface instances** to ensure driver state machines, bit serialization, monitor sampling/shift registers, decoded bytes, and scoreboard expected-versus-actual comparisons appear directly in **Synopsys Verdi waveforms** (`soc_uvm.fsdb`).
